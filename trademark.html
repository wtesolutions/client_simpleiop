Usage Guidelines

  • Always write the name as one word: SimplyIOP — "Simply" followed by the capitalized initials IOP (Increased Offertory Program).
  • On first mention in any document, spell out the meaning: "SimplyIOP — the Increased Offertory Program."
  • Use the primary mark on light backgrounds and the reversed mark on dark backgrounds.
  • Do not stretch, recolor, add effects to, or separate the icon from the wordmark.
  • Consultant partners may use the mark only within approved co-branded pages.
